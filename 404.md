# Oi da! Siden er borte.
That means *the page is gone*. It has probably gone *på tur* (on a hike), like every Norwegian on a sunny Sunday. While you're here: in **borte** the *rt* melts into one [retroflex](/retroflex/) sound. Now *gå hjem* – [go home](/) – and try again.
